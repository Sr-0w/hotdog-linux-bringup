## Runtime

The full module tree was hash-checked on the writable rootfs. The AVB image
was written only to `boot_b` and its complete 100,663,296-byte prefix was read
back and matched before reboot. The phone returned to SSH on the new boot in
21 seconds without a key sequence and without exposing Qualcomm 9008 or 900e.

The camera clock omission was resolved exactly as predicted:

- `ad00000.clock-controller` bound to `camcc-sm8150`;
- both CCI platform devices bound to `i2c-qcom-cci`;
- CAMSS bound and exposed one media device, eight video nodes and twenty
  subdevices;
- IMX586, IMX481, IMX471 and S5K3M5 loaded;
- both LC898217XC focus actuators reported ready;
- the popup power domain reported ready while leaving the motor off;
- no camera device remained deferred.

At the same time SLPI, modem and ADSP were all running, QRTR service 400 was
present, and the two Hall sensors plus `elliptic_proximity` remained exposed
through IIO. The preceding r7 boot had already held this remoteproc and sensor
state for 30 samples over 300 seconds with no SLPI crash after the FastRPC
sensors-PD heap fix.

The r8 boot then passed its own 30-sample, 300-second monitor. Every sample
retained the same boot identity, one media device, eight video nodes, twenty
subdevices, all three remote processors in `running` state and QRTR service
400 present.

## A/B hypothesis

A read-only `qbootctl` check reports slot B active, successful and bootable;
slot A is also successful and bootable. Repeated direct writes of `boot_b` did
not exhaust an A/B retry counter. The earlier r4 first-boot failure followed
by a successful reset of the same image is therefore not explained by an
unbootable slot marker.

## Scope

`PASS` for complete electronic kernel enumeration. This does not claim camera
capture quality, autofocus movement, popup movement, flash synchronization or
other physical behavior. Those interactions remain reserved for the final
physical parity pass after the complete migrated stack is stable.
