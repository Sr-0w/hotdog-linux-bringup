## Decoder

`scripts/decode_ap_stm_etf.py` implements the narrow decode required here:

1. split the 160-byte capture into 16-byte formatted CoreSight frames;
2. reconstruct per-ATID payload streams from the formatter ID/sideband bytes;
3. decode STPv2 nibbles in protocol order, low nibble then high nibble;
4. handle only the packet types generated by this smoke path: ASYNC, VERSION,
   FREQ, M8, C16, D8/D32 with or without timestamp, FLAG and NULL.

No local `trc_pkt_lister`, `cs_trace_decoder`, `cs-trace-disasm`, OpenCSD CLI or
`perf` decoder was available in `PATH` for this capture, so the helper keeps the
implemented subset explicit and covered by synthetic tests.

Usage:

```sh
python3 scripts/decode_ap_stm_etf.py \
  /home/srobin/dev/hotdog-r6-rebaseline/logs/2026-08-20-sensors-slpi-50-explicit-tmc-etf0-ap-smoke/37-ap-etf-capture.bin \
  --expect-sha256 a2f2e80fbf802b0f2dee6aa91d0b5b393f860ef10ca967f3a95ea26fe42296a7 \
  --expect-marker 'HOTDOG_STM_AP_SMOKE 20260820T201723Z pid=4622'
```

The reconstructed payload is exactly:

```text
HOTDOG_STM_AP_SMOKE 20260820T201723Z pid=4622
```

including the trailing line feed. The split before the line feed is consistent
with two userspace writes through `p_basic`, each followed by a FLAG.

The configured policy was software master 1/channel 10. The decoded channel
contains low byte `0x0a`, matching channel 10. The decoded master is `0x40`
because the Qualcomm STM driver uses hardware override for master IDs.

## Verdict

The S50 160-byte ETF capture is not empty. The AP marker is present with high
confidence after removing ETF framing and decoding STPv2. Literal `grep` or
`strings` on the raw capture is invalid because payload bytes are carried in
formatted CoreSight frames and STPv2 nibble encoding.
